# Doctor Photos Folder

Place doctor profile photos (e.g., `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.webp`) in this directory (`public/doctors/`).

## How to use photos:
1. **Excel/JSON Upload**: In your Excel spreadsheet or JSON file, include a `Photo` column containing the filename of the photo inside this folder (e.g. `dr_ayesha_khan.jpg`) or a full URL.
2. **Access Path**: Files placed here are accessible at `/doctors/your_photo_name.jpg`.

## Example:
| Date | DoctorName | Specialty | Timing | Photo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-07-29 | Dr. Ayesha Khan | General Physician | 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM | dr_ayesha_khan.jpg |
